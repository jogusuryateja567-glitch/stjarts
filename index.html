Begin Your Commission ↘
01

Share Your Photograph

Send your cherished pictures via WhatsApp. Even older, faded, or slightly blurred photos are carefully restored and aligned before any pencil touches paper.

02

Composition Proof

A tonal proportion layout and sample preview are shared with you. You approve the angle, crop, and expressive lighting prior to final shading.

03

Handmade Creation

Layer upon layer of graphite, blending stump, and charcoal powder are developed by hand. Typically 2 days per face of continuous, patient craft.

04

Archival Framing & Ship

Once you inspect and wholeheartedly approve the final portrait, the artwork is sealed with archival fixative, custom framed, and insured for nationwide transit.
